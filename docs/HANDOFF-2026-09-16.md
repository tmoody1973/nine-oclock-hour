## The new project: Nine O'Clock Hour

A newsroom simulation built from the day's real CDS wire. A listener fills the 9 a.m. hour, presses air, and gets an aircheck.

**Prototype (working, published):** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/1f862257-15ae-4c65-8f36-b0550381219a — version 4. Source lives at `/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-tarikmoody-Projects-hyfin-grove/2c8ef1f8-3e15-494f-af26-0da4996f3beb/scratchpad/newsroom-sim/index.html`. **Copy it out of the scratchpad before that session's files are cleaned up.**

**What the prototype already does:** six newsrooms (88Nine, KCRW, WBEZ, WNYC, WABE, KQED) each with a neighbour whose tape may not be rolled; roll-tape versus 30-second read; the newscast expiry trap; untimed Chicago tape that only reveals its length on air; immovable weather (:19) and traffic (:49) windows; an underwriting credit that must clear by :30; a bulletin at :34 with three ways to take it; pledge week; the exit-ramp rule; and a five-part aircheck (Clock 30, On air 25, Freshness 15, Mix 15, Hold 15) with a modelled retention line.

**Design corrections Tarik made, worth not repeating:**
- The first version flagged eight rights "violations" in one hour using CDS licensing vocabulary. Wrong model. The game now describes the *plant*: network tape comes down the satellite and is yours to air; another station's tape needs a phone call; podcast audio is not cleared for broadcast.
- Audio players embedded in an artifact **cannot work** — the platform blocks media from other hosts. That is why the real build goes to Vercel.
- The story modal needs real context, not a one-line teaser. The plan generates summaries at build time rather than pasting story text, which cannot be redistributed.

**The plan to build it properly:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-16-hour-player.md` in this repo (`~/Projects/newsroom-sim`, git initialised, no commits or remote yet). Nine tasks: Next.js skeleton, server-side CDS reads, the day-file builder, a 5 a.m. Vercel cron writing to Blob, the rules engine ported as a pure module, the player (one audio element, Media Session, lock-screen controls), the personal retention meter with an end card against the real Morning Edition rundown, the deploy, and voicing the reads.

**Voice decision (2026-09-16):** reads are spoken by Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS. It is $10 per million audio tokens at 25 tokens per second of audio, about 1.5¢ a minute, against ElevenLabs at roughly 17¢ a minute by their own table; a dozen 30-second reads a day costs around 9¢. ElevenLabs stays on the list for a cloned 88Nine host voice later, which is a branding purchase rather than a saving. Scripts are our own summaries with the source named aloud — never a publisher's copy performed, which display-only rights do not allow. Needs `GEMINI_API_KEY` in Vercel.

**Decided:** Phaser and pixel art come *after* the loop holds people; the player is the product. spritecook is the intended art tool, with original characters only.

**Known CDS ids**, confirmed live today: All Things Considered = collection `2`; Morning Edition = `3`; NPR News Now newscast = podcast channel `500005`; member-station local pieces carry collection `319418027`. Owners: 88Nine `s921`, KCRW `s55`, WBEZ `s308`, WNYC `s552`, WABE `s295`, KQED `s150`.

**Rights, which shape the design:** store metadata only, never audio. NPR premium audio is play-or-link-never-store. Other stations' items are display-only: attribute by url, refresh regularly. WBEZ and KQED file audio with **no duration**, which became the untimed-tape rule. WNYC and WABE file text only.
